    What to know about this role

    Weekend availability is part of the role

    This is a part-time store opening, and weekend availability is important for the team schedule. If weekends work well for you, this role is more likely to be a strong match.

    We share pay early and clearly

    This role currently starts at $13.73 per hour. Team members are also eligible for biannual reviews and raises up to 5%, so we want applicants to have a clear picture of both the starting rate and the opportunity to grow.

    Pet passion matters, and so does store work

    This role combines helping pet parents with cashier work, stocking, cleaning, lifting, and general support around the store.
