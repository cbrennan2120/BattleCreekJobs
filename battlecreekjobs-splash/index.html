Special edition

Help wanted is going local again.

Battle Creek Jobs is being built to match local people with local jobs without the cost, clutter, and complexity of large national hiring tools.

Big platforms like Indeed can be expensive for employers, harder to manage, and often produce poor matches between applicants and real openings. Battle Creek Jobs aims to bring hiring back closer to home.

More featured employers and more local openings are coming soon.
